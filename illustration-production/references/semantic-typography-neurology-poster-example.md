## Filled Prompt

Create a 1:1 modern flat geometric typography poster based on the word `Neurology`.

Language: English.

Context: clinical neurology, localization, signal, networks, bedside exam, anatomy and meaning.

Emotional direction: precise, calm, structured, professional, intellectually clear.

Design the word `NEUROLOGY` as a modular visual system. The word should become the backbone of the poster, with node-link pathways, transparent diagnostic boxes, grid structure, and signal routes expressing clinical localization and network thinking.

Use a clean clinical palette: warm white, Emory deep blue, silver blue, aqua cyan, small ochre accents. Keep the composition crisp, vector-like, and readable.

Avoid realistic brain renderings, glossy sci-fi neural networks, excessive data labels, dark cyberpunk lighting, and illegible experimental type.
