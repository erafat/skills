# CV Eligibility Rules

Use these rules when deciding whether a candidate activity can be proposed for a
CV update.

## Default Include

Include candidates when at least one is true:

- publication is published, online ahead of print, indexed, accepted, or has a
  stable DOI/PMID/public journal page
- abstract/poster/talk is accepted, presented, or listed in a public program or
  official abstract database
- lecture, invited talk, workshop, grand rounds, or course session was delivered
  or publicly scheduled with the user as speaker/faculty
- teaching material, software, media, podcast, website, or educational artifact
  is public and attributable to the user
- mentoring, supervision, observership, or trainee collaboration is active,
  completed, or formally documented, with enough evidence to identify the
  mentee, role, institution, and years supervised
- service, committee, leadership, mentorship, reviewing, editorial, grant, award,
  or institutional activity is completed or formally appointed/accepted

## Default Exclude

Exclude or hold candidates when any is true:

- planned but not delivered
- drafted but not submitted
- submitted but not accepted
- internal-only project work without a completed public, accepted, or formally
  appointed milestone
- routine recurring output when the profile says to keep only major milestones
- insufficient evidence for title, date, role, or venue
- project output is not completed/public/accepted, unless the only proposed CV
  item is a separately confirmed mentoring or supervision relationship
- contains patient identifiers or private details that should not enter a CV

## Category Placement

Classify each candidate by activity type before choosing a CV section. Do not
place items by title match alone.

- journal publications belong in manuscripts or published research articles
- talks, lectures, and conference presentations belong in the relevant
  presentation or lecture section
- public educational media and websites belong in teaching aids, digital
  scholarship, public media, or the closest existing local section
- mentees, observers, students, fellows, and supervised junior collaborators
  belong in Mentoring Activities or the closest existing supervision section

When a manuscript and a talk share a similar or identical title, treat them as
separate candidates and verify both locations independently.

## Borderline Labels

Use one of these labels in the packet:

- `eligible`: completed/public/accepted with enough evidence
- `hold_for_confirmation`: likely eligible but section, wording, or evidence is
  not confirmed
- `not_now`: real activity but not yet completed/public/accepted
- `exclude`: not CV-appropriate or explicitly outside the profile rules

## Confidence

- `High`: direct public or local evidence verifies date, title, role, and venue
- `Medium`: activity is supported but section placement or official wording is
  uncertain
- `Low`: activity may be real but evidence is incomplete

Low-confidence candidates must not be inserted into the Word CV without explicit
user approval and visible caveat handling.
