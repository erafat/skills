# Readiness Checklist

## Coauthor-Ready

A draft is coauthor-ready when:

- the main argument is coherent
- known major reviewer concerns are addressed or flagged
- claims are not obviously overextended
- references are usable enough for coauthor review
- remaining asks are clear

Coauthor-ready does not mean submission-final.

## Submission-Final

A draft is submission-final only when:

- all required files are current
- reviewer responses match actual manuscript changes
- references and citations have been checked
- word count and journal requirements are satisfied
- figures/tables are final or explicitly marked as final
- final human review is complete

## Final Answer Pattern

State one of:

- Coauthor-ready
- Near-final but not submission-final
- Not ready

Then list only the blockers that change the next action.
