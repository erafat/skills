# cozy-hand-drawn-storybook-cartoon

温馨手绘童书卡通画风 - Cozy hand-drawn storybook cartoon for reassuring education comics

## Overview

A cozy, non-realistic storybook cartoon style tuned for health education and waiting-room explainers. Compared with `storybook-watercolor`, this style keeps similarly warm and gentle rendering but uses clearer cartoon linework for stronger readability in panel sequences.

## Line Work

- Slightly wobbly hand-inked outlines (retain hand-drawn feel)
- More defined outer contours than `storybook-watercolor`
- Subtle line-weight variation; avoid rigid vector-perfect lines
- Keep interior detail minimal and readable (no realistic wrinkles/pores)

## Character Design

- Friendly simplified proportions (slightly larger heads, soft silhouette shapes)
- Simplified facial features (dot/oval eyes, minimal nose and mouth lines)
- Reassuring expressions and open body language
- Preserve identity lock: hair silhouette, eye color, outfit palette, baseline skin tone

## Background Treatment

- Calm, uncluttered environments; prioritize clarity over detail
- Use simple props and clear silhouettes
- Maintain generous negative space for text and visual breathing room

## Color & Rendering

- Warm paper texture base (subtle, never noisy)
- Flat-to-soft gouache fills with controlled texture
- Minimal shading (one soft shadow tone max), no glossy highlights
- Avoid cinematic lighting, hard contrast, and 3D-render look

### Suggested Palette

| Role | Color | Hex |
|------|-------|-----|
| Paper | Warm cream | #F6F0E3 |
| Primary Accent | Gentle coral | #E58C72 |
| Secondary Accent | Soft teal | #6FA5A1 |

| Line | Soft charcoal | #2E2D2B |

## Typography

- Use plain printed SANS-SERIF for all dialogue and labels
- Keep labels short; if unreadable, leave blank rather than garbling
- Prefer larger text and high contrast against background

## Quality Markers

- ✓ Clearly hand-drawn and cartoon-like (not realistic)
- ✓ Warm, reassuring atmosphere suitable for sensitive topics
- ✓ Readable panel flow with uncluttered compositions
- ✓ Consistent character identity across all pages
- ✓ Minimal detail and shading with gentle gouache texture
