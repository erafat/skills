# storybook-watercolor

童书水彩画风 - Cozy children's-book / editorial cartoon look

## Overview

A warm, friendly, simplified illustration style commonly seen in modern children's books and gentle editorial cartoons. Prioritizes approachability and emotional clarity over realism — ideal for patient education and reassurance. Characters are adults with natural proportions; warmth comes from color and line quality, not exaggeration.

## Line Work

- Slightly wobbly hand-inked outline (not perfect vector lines)
- Subtle line-weight variation (thicker outer contour, lighter interior details)
- Minimal internal detail lines (avoid realistic wrinkles/pores)
- Clean panel borders; white gutters

## Character Design

- Natural adult proportions (NOT enlarged heads or "cute" chibi distortion)
- EXTREMELY simplified facial features: small dot eyes, tiny dot or absent nose, small line mouth — NO eyebrows, NO eyelids, NO lip detail, NO wrinkles
- **Character identity = SILHOUETTE + CLOTHING COLOR BLOCKS + HAIR SHAPE** — not facial detail
- **Emotion through BODY LANGUAGE and posture** — not facial expression
- Keep identity consistent across panels: same hair silhouette, clothing colors, skin tone watercolor wash

## Background Treatment

- Simplified environments (few props; clear silhouettes)
- Soft perspective; no hyper-detailed textures
- Use negative space and calm shapes to reduce visual noise
- Warm parchment paper texture (#F0E6D0) as base — visible grain, slight aged quality

## Color & Rendering

- Soft watercolor wash fills on warm parchment paper texture
- Slight color bleed at edges; soft boundaries between fills (not airbrushed, not flat digital)
- Minimal shading (one gentle shadow tone max); no glossy highlights
- Warm neutral palette; avoid high-contrast "cinematic" lighting
- Skin tones as watercolor washes — warm olive, warm tan, rich dark brown — NOT flat fills

## Typography

- Prefer large, plain printed SANS-SERIF for legibility (especially for Gemini models)
- Keep labels short; if text might be unreadable, leave blank rather than garble
- Narrator boxes: rounded rectangle, warm cream fill, soft charcoal outline
- Speech bubbles: clean oval, white fill, soft charcoal outline

## Quality Markers

- ✓ Reads as hand-drawn, cozy, and non-threatening
- ✓ Warm parchment texture present but subtle
- ✓ Minimal facial detail — dot eyes only, NO eyebrows, NO eyelids
- ✓ Soft watercolor wash feel (gentle edges, slight bleed; not airbrushed)
- ✓ Calm palette and uncluttered compositions
- ✓ Character recognizable by silhouette + color blocks alone, not face
- ✓ Adult proportions maintained throughout
